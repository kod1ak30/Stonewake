# Stonewake progression roadmap

The playable game ends at Keep level 10. Troops, buildings, commanders, ships and provinces are also capped at level 10. This build does not add later-age content.

| Levels | Planned setting | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–10 | Medieval coastal kingdom | Growing towns, defenses, trebuchets, armies and naval support |
| 10–20 | Gunpowder age | Transition begins at level 10 in a future update; artillery, sailing fleets and civic institutions |
| 20–30 | Industrial and early 1900s | Rail, factories, early mechanized armies and steel navies |
| 30–40 | Modern day | Modern cities, combined arms and advanced infrastructure |
| 40–50 | Future | Speculative technology, future cities and fleets |

Level 10 remains the current cap until the gunpowder transition is designed and implemented. Existing prototype cannons and bombard ships remain available; they do not unlock an implemented post-10 era.

## Current city loop

Resident deliveries exchange surplus resources for useful rewards. Wells increase production; storehouses hold more resources; workshops craft goods and reduce construction times; taverns improve resident rewards and host temporary production festivals. Requests refresh after a short cooldown. No streak penalty or paid purchase is required.

Computer coastal raids use the player's actual layout. At Keep 3, a twenty-minute initial shield is followed by a five-minute warning. Each settlement grants a two-hour protection period. Returning after a long absence resolves at most one overdue raid. Walls and area defenses protect exposed stores; a held Keep earns a bounded bounty and two gems. Twenty percent of storage capacity is protected, and losses are capped at eighteen percent of the exposed portion. The city and home army recover. Reports show measured contributions from walls and towers.

Four civic districts develop the market, waterfront, residential quarter and public gardens through three stages each. They occupy normal construction crews, visibly improve existing sites and add permanent production, construction or fleet benefits. See BUILD11-PROGRESSION.md for requirements and verification.

Cloud backup, separate server-owned online kingdoms, asynchronous player battles and verified StoreKit gem purchases are implemented for service activation. Hosting authentication, Apple capability and product provisioning, and real Sandbox verification remain required. The service URL is empty until those steps are complete, and the local game remains available offline. See ONLINE-DEPLOYMENT.md.

Naval support remains in battle until victory, retreat, time limit or sinking. Enemy ships and coastal weapons can destroy it. Wrecked vessels return to the shipyard for resource-funded repair without losing their upgrade level.
