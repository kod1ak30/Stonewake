# Stonewake build 14

Build 14 is installed and launched on the paired iPhone 15 Pro Max. Existing village progress was preserved. The current gem balance is 1,023, including two gems earned by an overdue automatic defense when the game resumed.

## Two ways to campaign

**Land** keeps direct perimeter deployment without needing ships. **Sea** has ten separate chapters built around coastal assaults, convoy escorts and blockades. Each campaign keeps its own progress and first-clear rewards.

Sea preparation has three steps: choose your force, choose owned ships, and assign cargo. Troops consume different amounts of space. “Fit force to ships” reduces an oversized force before departure, and cargo can be adjusted manually. Choose landing locations, protect transports, direct fire and withdraw damaged ships during battle.

Four ship roles are available: Coastal cutter, Landing cog, War galley and Siege barge. Upgrades change capacity or combat performance and visible equipment. Enemy ships and coastal defenses can sink your vessels. Wrecked ships remain yours and can be repaired; troops still aboard are rescued.

## Easier daily play

- Army selection uses six families with doctrines and saved forces. “Train missing” checks the combined cost and space before spending.
- Drag directly from a troop card onto valid ground, or select a card and tap, hold or paint along the deployment area. Pinching interrupts deployment safely.
- Keep and Storehouse details show the full upgrade route, including missing buildings, capacity and supplies. The second Storehouse appears first when it is needed.
- Menus use a consistent dark style, clearer Back/Close controls and layouts for portrait and short landscape. Next missions and ships needing attention come first.
- Layout mode supports road painting, moving, rotating, undo/redo and commit/cancel, with overlays for roads, plots, defenses and the harbor.

## Presentation

Troops and ships use shared articulated animation with equipment changes through level 10. Battle effects include material damage, collapse, rubble, smoke, water impacts and wakes. Roads connect visually, and citizens carry out visible work. Music defaults are quieter, with separate village and battle sliders.

The Journal gathers story tasks, civic activity, mastery challenges, collections and battle reports. Existing cosmetics and purchases are preserved; the previous 1,000-gem test grant is not repeated.

## What was tested

280 rule checks, 27 gesture checks, 12 backend tests, twenty naval feasibility simulations, two manually played browser sea victories, replay, layout tools and the main menus at three phone sizes passed. The native app was checked in portrait and both landscape directions in Simulator. Actual phone build number, launch and save migration were verified.

Physical touch feel, audibility, haptics and sustained device performance still need hands-on validation. These checks do not establish final commercial art quality or complete gameplay balance. Production online services and real-money purchases remain unactivated. All playable progression remains medieval, through level 10.
