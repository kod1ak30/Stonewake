# Build 15 art provenance and scope

Built-in image generation was used for two original assets. Reference art was the project's existing `ships-v13.png`; reference designs were not copied. Generated originals were retained in the local image output folder.

| Asset | Purpose | Layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Stonewake/Web/art/build15/coastal-ships.png` | Landing cog and medieval trebuchet barge upgrades, used in the world and previews | 1536×1024 RGBA; 3 columns, 2 rows. Top: cog tiers 1/4/7. Bottom: barge tiers 1/4/7. |
| `Stonewake/Web/art/build15/harbor-dawn.png` | Original welcome illustration | 1536×1024; cinematic coastal kingdom and harbor. |

The ship output's alpha was verified from the PNG: transparent gutters and corners, with full-color ship silhouettes. No background-removal script or external editing service was used. The generator preview displayed RGB colors under transparent areas, so a second background-removal generation was performed before checking the alpha channel directly.

## Prompt record

Ship prompt: original premium hand-painted isometric medieval ship atlas matching the camera, material richness and restrained realism of the existing Stonewake ship art. Exactly three columns and two rows. Top row: humble, reinforced and ornate landing cogs with oak hulls, open troop decks, boarding ramps and teal sails. Bottom row: simple, reinforced and ornate wide siege barges with functional counterweight trebuchets, stone ammunition, braces and teal rear sails. No gunpowder weapons, text, labels, water or background. Consistent lower-left bows and upper-left illumination. A second edit requested removal of all backdrop while preserving all six ships, placement and design.

Welcome prompt: original medieval coastal kingdom at dawn, limestone castle with blue-teal roofs and cream falcon banners above timber docks, oak cogs with teal sails, small residents, a smith, mill, cottages and cobbled paths. Warm golden sunlight, turquoise water, distant sea stacks and a hopeful maritime atmosphere. No text, logos, UI or copied characters. Elevated cinematic composition whose middle 55% supports a tall panel crop.

## Remaining art production

Existing character atlases contain a limited number of authored poses and reflected viewpoints. Fitted equipment changes improve progression, but do not constitute ten fully authored animated models per troop. A final art-rights inventory and human visual approval are still release gates. Generated art does not replace that review of the entire pre-existing asset/audio library.
